# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Korean Coupang sellers, beginner private sellers, and small operators who need to estimate Rocket Growth costs before buying inventory or setting a selling price. They usually need quick answers, plain Korean explanations, and practical examples rather than accounting-grade reports.

## Product Purpose

Rocket Growth Calculator helps sellers estimate China sourcing, China-to-Korea logistics, Korea-to-Coupang inbound costs, Coupang selling costs, final unit cost, margin, and minimum ROAS before committing money. The community layer keeps users engaged with seller tips, cost cases, Q&A, and reusable reference content.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, plainspoken.

## Anti-references

Do not feel like a flashy marketing landing page, a generic SaaS dashboard, or a developer demo. Avoid unfinished calculator tabs, vague "coming soon" surfaces, hidden SEO text, noisy cards, and explanations that sound like legal or tax advice.

## Design Principles

1. Calculation first, explanation second.
2. Every input should make its effect visible quickly.
3. Beginner sellers need examples, not jargon.
4. Community content should extend the calculator workflow, not distract from it.
5. Public guidance must stay clear that figures are estimates and not official tax, customs, or Coupang policy confirmation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable Korean typography, strong input affordances, keyboard-focusable controls, reduced-motion fallbacks, and mobile layouts without text overlap. The interface should remain usable for sellers who are comparing costs while reading quotes or invoices on another screen.
