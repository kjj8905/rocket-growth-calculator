## Role

MVP2 explores a workspace where the brand title and content navigation stay in a persistent left rail, while the calculator and seller content sit on the right.

## Structure

- Left dark rail with product title and navigation
- Right-side calculator explanation/home content
- Vertical five-stage banner list on home
- Stage view keeps the left rail and places the calculator workspace on the right

## Design Intent

Document/community desk. This version is useful if the site should feel more like a seller knowledge workspace than a pure calculator.
